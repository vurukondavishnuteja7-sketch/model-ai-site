Contact MODEL AI

Tell us what needs improvement. Clear reproduction steps make it easier to understand a tool or content issue.

What we can help with

Contact MODEL AI to report a broken tool, unclear instructions, an accessibility problem, an incorrect guide or a suggestion for a future feature.

Include useful details

  • The page title and full URL.
  • The input type you used, without sharing private content.
  • What you expected the tool to do and what happened instead.
  • Your browser and device if the issue is visual or related to downloading a file.
  • A screenshot only if it helps; remove personal information, passwords and API keys first.

Contact email

Email: hello@modelaitools.online

For a productive response, write a clear subject such as “Image Generator error” or “Guide correction”. Do not email API secrets, account passwords, government identifiers or confidential documents.

Corrections to guides

If you spot a factual or calculation error, mention the section heading and include a reliable reference where possible. For institution-specific grading rules, identify the institution and official page so the guide can distinguish a general example from an official formula.
