About this tool

Resize images locally with contain, cover-crop or stretch options. The source image remains unchanged.

Fit modes explained

  • Contain: keeps the whole image visible inside the requested canvas, which may leave empty space.
  • Cover / crop: fills the requested dimensions and trims the edges around the centre.
  • Stretch: fills the exact dimensions but can distort the subject.

Choose the mode that suits the target placement and review the downloaded output carefully.
