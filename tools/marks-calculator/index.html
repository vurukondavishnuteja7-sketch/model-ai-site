About this tool

Calculate totals and percentages from marks you enter. Use a single maximum mark for every subject, or provide a matching maximum for each subject.

Example calculation

If four subjects have marks of 78, 85, 92 and 66, each out of 100, the total is 321 out of 400 and the percentage is 80.25%. If subjects have different maximum marks, enter each maximum in the same order as its mark.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Do not mix marks obtained with grade points.
  • Check that maximum marks match the subject list and that no obtained mark exceeds its maximum.
  • Use your school’s official rounding rules when recording final results.
