About this tool

Background removal usually needs a dedicated segmentation model or external service and may incur costs. This starter does not upload your image or claim to remove its background until you configure an endpoint.

Privacy and cost

Background removal needs a model or provider that can segment foreground objects. External providers may charge per image and may process or retain uploaded files under their own policies. This starter does not upload the chosen image and does not simulate removal.

Before enabling it

Choose a provider, read its terms, set usage limits and connect it through a server-side endpoint. Then test hair, transparent objects, shadows and complex backgrounds.
