About this tool

Turn a list of subjects into a practical study timetable. The generated plan is a starting point; adjust it for breaks, exam dates and the subjects you find hardest.

Make the timetable realistic

Use shorter sessions for difficult topics, reserve breaks, and leave time for homework and revision. A generated timetable is only a draft; it does not know your school schedule, energy levels or upcoming exams.

Study planning tip

Try to alternate subjects that require different kinds of thinking. Review yesterday’s material briefly before moving to a new topic, and revise your plan weekly instead of treating it as fixed.
