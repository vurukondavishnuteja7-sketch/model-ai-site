About this tool

Create a local flashcard deck for revision. Cards stay in the current browser session and are not sent to a server. AI card generation can be enabled after the secure AI backend is configured.

Use active recall

Good flashcards test one idea at a time. Keep the front focused on one question and the back short enough to review quickly. For long textbook paragraphs, turn separate facts, definitions or cause-and-effect relationships into separate cards.

Privacy note

This starter keeps the cards in the current page session. Refreshing or closing the page can clear the deck; export and cloud syncing are not included in this first version.
