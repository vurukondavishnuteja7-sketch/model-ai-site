About this tool

Calculate the duration between two calendar dates using your browser. Results are informational and not a substitute for legal age verification.

What the result means

The tool compares calendar dates and reports elapsed days plus a year/month/day breakdown. Time zones and time-of-day are not used in this date-only calculation, and the result should not be used as legal proof of age.

Leap years

Calendar differences can involve leap years and months of different lengths. For official eligibility or legal deadlines, verify the applicable local rule rather than relying only on a date calculator.
