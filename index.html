✦ Make more of your workspace

Quick guidance for using MODEL AI thoughtfully

Study smarter

Use calculators to check arithmetic, build a study timetable around your real deadlines, and turn your own notes into flashcards. Verify important answers against your class materials.

Create with intention

Start with a clear goal, add your audience and preferred style, then review generated titles, scripts and writing before publishing. Use creator tools as a starting point rather than a substitute for your own judgement.

Handle files carefully

Many utilities are designed to run in your browser. Keep a copy of important originals and check converted files before sharing. AI-enabled features may send the input you submit to the configured provider.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use MODEL AI without an account?

Many browser-based tools can be used without signing in. Signing in is intended for account-related functionality; some AI features depend on provider configuration.

Are all AI features free and unlimited?

No. Some tools work locally in the browser, while AI chat and image generation depend on third-party providers, their current free tiers, quotas, availability and pricing.

Are my prompts and files stored?

Browser-based tools process inputs locally where applicable. When you submit a request to an AI feature, the relevant text or image may be sent to the configured AI provider. Do not submit sensitive information.

How can I report a problem?

Visit the Contact page and include the tool name, what you expected, what happened and your browser/device. Remove personal details, passwords and API keys from any screenshot.
