Privacy Policy

This policy explains the categories of information that may be processed when you use browser-based tools, account features and AI-enabled workflows.

Scope of this policy

This policy describes the intended data handling for MODEL AI, a website that offers browser-based utilities and optional AI-powered features. The exact behaviour of a feature depends on the current deployment and its configured providers. Please read the notice shown on an individual tool and do not submit information you would not want processed by that tool.

Information you provide

When you use a calculator, formatter or other browser-only tool, inputs are designed to be processed locally where the feature supports local processing. Your browser may keep preferences such as theme, favourites, recent tools and local chat history in local storage so they remain available on that device. Clearing site data in your browser can remove these local preferences and locally saved conversations.

If you create an account or sign in, Firebase Authentication processes sign-in information to authenticate you. If cloud chat history is enabled and the necessary Firestore configuration is active, conversation text may be saved under your authenticated account. Local saved history can also be available without signing in. Uploaded images and files are not included in local chat-history text snapshots, but this does not mean that they are never sent when you submit them to an AI tool.

AI providers and uploaded files

When you submit a prompt to AI Chat or an image-analysis feature, the relevant text and supported file content may be sent through the site’s server-side API route to the external provider configured for that feature. The current deployment is designed to support OpenRouter for text/chat and vision requests, Gemini for image generation, and an optional third-party service for background removal. The active providers may change. Those providers process submitted information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Do not submit passwords, payment details, government identifiers, confidential work, private school records or another person’s information unless you have a clear reason and permission. For sensitive material, prefer an offline tool and verify whether the chosen tool truly runs locally before use.

API keys and security

Provider API keys should be stored as encrypted server-side secrets in Cloudflare Pages, not in public HTML, browser JavaScript, screenshots or a public repository. We aim to limit request size and rate, but no internet service can promise absolute security. Do not share your account credentials or API keys in support messages.

Cookies, local storage and similar technologies

The site can use browser local storage for preferences, favourites, recent tools and local chat history. Firebase sign-in may use browser storage or other mechanisms needed to maintain an authentication session. If analytics, advertising or additional third-party services are enabled, they may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own documentation and your settings.

Advertising and AdSense

Ad placement areas may be reserved in the interface, but a reserved space is not proof that ads are active. If Google AdSense or another advertising service is activated, this policy must be updated to identify the actual advertising providers and describe the cookies, personalised advertising choices and consent controls applicable to visitors. Users may be able to manage ad personalisation through the controls offered by the relevant provider. Applicable regional consent requirements must be implemented before serving ads where required.

Children and student users

Use the website with appropriate adult guidance where required. Do not enter personal details into prompts or upload another person’s document without permission. Privacy practices and advertising controls may differ by region and age group; the service should only be used in ways permitted by applicable rules.

Retention and your choices

Local preferences and local chat history can generally be removed by clearing this website’s stored data in your browser. If cloud conversation sync is enabled, cloud copies are managed separately from local storage. Cloud conversation copies are separate from browser-local history. If you need help with account data, use the contact channel listed below and include the account email without sending your password.

Third-party links

Links to external tools and services are provided for convenience. Their availability, security and privacy practices are controlled by the respective providers, not by this website. Review a provider’s policies before creating an account or sending information.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, contact hello@modelaitools.online. This policy may be revised when providers, analytics, advertising, account features or data practices change.
